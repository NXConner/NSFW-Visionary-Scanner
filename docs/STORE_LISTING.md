**GrowthTracker - Your Private Men's Health Companion**

Take control of your personal health journey with GrowthTracker, the most comprehensive and private men's health tracking app available. Designed with medical-grade security and powered by AI analysis, GrowthTracker helps you monitor, understand, and improve your health over time.

**🔒 PRIVACY FIRST**
Your health data stays on YOUR device. No cloud uploads, no third-party access, no data selling. All measurements and health records are encrypted with military-grade AES-256 encryption and stored locally.

**📊 COMPREHENSIVE TRACKING**
• Visual health scanning with AR measurement guides
• Detailed health diary with calendar view
• Progress photos with side-by-side comparison
• Curvature angle documentation and monitoring
• Multiple measurement metrics tracking

**🤖 AI-POWERED INSIGHTS**
• Intelligent scan analysis for health indicators
• Predictive analytics for progress forecasting
• Personalized health recommendations
• AI health chatbot for questions and guidance

**📈 PROGRESS MONITORING**
• Interactive charts showing trends over time
• Goal setting with milestone tracking
• Weekly and monthly progress reports
• Comprehensive scan history comparison

**🏥 MEDICAL FEATURES**
• Peyronie's Disease self-assessment
• Visual health detection for multiple conditions
• Emergency guidance with severity levels
• Physician locator for specialists
• Export data in medical formats (HL7 FHIR, CDA)

**💪 ENHANCEMENT GUIDE**
• Comprehensive PE methods education
• Experience-level filtered routines
• Safety guidelines and best practices
• Routine builder for personalized programs

**🔔 SMART REMINDERS**
• Medication tracking notifications

• Weekly progress report alerts
• Customizable notification schedules

**♿ ACCESSIBILITY**
• Dark and light theme support

• Haptic feedback controls

**📱 WORKS OFFLINE**
